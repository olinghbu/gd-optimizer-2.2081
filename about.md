# GD Optimizer

Makes demanding levels run smoother.

**Inside a level:**
- Forces **Low Detail Mode** (your own LDM choice is restored when you leave)
- Removes **object glow**
- Removes **level particles**
- Ignores **shader triggers** (blur, glitch, shockwave...), which redraw the whole screen

**On your PC (Windows):**
- Lowers the priority of background programs (Discord, Steam, browsers...) and raises Geometry Dash to High priority, restoring everything when you leave

Each option can be turned off in the mod settings. Changes apply the next time you enter a level.
